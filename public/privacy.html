What we store

  • In your browser: your progress and, if you sign in, a login token. This uses your browser's local storage.
  • If you sign in: your username, a one-way hash of your PIN, and your progress (including the answers you write), so you can continue on any device.
  • Usage limits: to prevent abuse, we count AI mentor requests per username and per IP address for the current day. These counters are deleted after two days.

Don't put personal information in your username or answers.

The AI mentor

When you use the AI mentor, your answer and the recent conversation for that system are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate a reply. See Anthropic's privacy policy.

Advertising

This site shows ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can learn more at How Google uses information from sites that use its services, and opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ad Settings. Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown.
