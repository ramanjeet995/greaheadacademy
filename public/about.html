Gearhead Academy

What it is

Gearhead Academy teaches mechanical, electromechanical and robotics, electrical, civil and software engineering — and how the human body works — by guided discovery. Instead of reading how something works, you're asked how you would make it work — then your idea is compared with real designs, and you go one layer deeper.

How it works

Every system has five layers. They follow both an engineer's career and the machine's history:

  1. Student — the basic idea: the parts and how they connect.
  2. Junior — the core mechanism: which one, how it moves, and why.
  3. Mid-level — geometry, feel, wear and what goes wrong.
  4. Senior — assistance and safety in the real world.
  5. Modern — what engineers build today.

No maths is required. The focus is on methods and mechanisms: what the parts are, how they move, and why engineers chose them.

How lessons are written

Every lesson on Gearhead Academy — in every field, including topics you explore yourself — is written by AI and hasn't been reviewed by an expert yet. Each one is labelled. Treat them as a starting point and double-check anything important. Spotted a mistake? Tell us at the contact address below.

Human body & medicine lessons are for education only. They are not medical advice — for anything about your own health, talk to a doctor or other qualified professional.

The AI mentor

Signed-in learners get a few AI mentor replies each day. The mentor reads your answer, compares it with how real designs work, and asks the next question based on the mechanism you chose. Without it, you still see the real-world answer after each layer.
