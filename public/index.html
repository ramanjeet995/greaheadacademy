What is Gearhead Academy?

Gearhead Academy teaches engineering, software and how the human body works by guided discovery. Pick a topic — a car's steering, a suspension bridge, the power grid, a search engine, the heart — and you're asked how you would make it work. You answer in plain words. Your idea is compared with how real designs do it, and then you go one layer deeper: which mechanism, how it moves, what goes wrong, how engineers made it safe, and what they build today.

Each topic has five layers that track an engineer's career and the machine's history: Student (the basic idea), Junior (the core mechanism), Mid-level (geometry, feel and failure), Senior (assistance and safety) and Modern (what's built today).
