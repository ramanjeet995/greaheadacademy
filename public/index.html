What is Gearhead Academy?

Pick something — a car's steering, a suspension bridge, the power grid, a search engine, the heart — and work out how you'd make it yourself, in plain words with no maths. You compare your idea with how real designs do it, then go one layer deeper each time, from the first idea to what engineers build today. Machines, robots, circuits, structures, software and the human body are all covered.
