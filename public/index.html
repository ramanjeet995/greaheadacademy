What is Gearhead Academy?

Gearhead Academy teaches mechanical and electromechanical engineering by guided discovery. Pick a machine — a car's steering, its brakes, a gearbox, an elevator, a robot joint — and you're asked how you would make it work. You answer in plain words. Your idea is compared with how real designs do it, and then you go one layer deeper: which mechanism, how it moves, what goes wrong, how engineers made it safe, and what they build today.

Each system has five layers that track an engineer's career and the machine's history: Student (the basic idea), Junior (the core mechanism), Mid-level (geometry, feel and failure), Senior (assistance and safety) and Modern (what's built today).
